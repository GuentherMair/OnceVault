OnceVault

OnceVault — share a secret once, then it burns.

Your secret is encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM before anything leaves your machine. The server only ever stores the ciphertext under a random ID with an expiry you pick (1 hour to 7 days). The decryption key travels only in the #fragment of the share link, which browsers never send to any server — OnceVault couldn't read your secret even if it wanted to. The first person to open the link gets the secret; the ciphertext is deleted in the same moment. Expired secrets are deleted unread.

Durations: 1 hour, 4 hours, 8 hours, 1 day, 2 days, 5 days or 7 days. Whatever is not retrieved in time is deleted unread.

Read-once: the first open of a share link atomically fetches and deletes the ciphertext. Anyone opening the same link afterwards — including you — sees an error, never the secret.

  • Yellow — your share link, ready to copy and send.
  • Green — a secret decrypted for you, shown exactly once.
  • Red — an error: burned, expired, or an invalid key.

HTTPS required: the Web Crypto API only works in a secure context, so OnceVault must be served over HTTPS (or localhost for testing).
